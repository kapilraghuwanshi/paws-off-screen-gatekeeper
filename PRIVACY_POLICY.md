# Privacy Policy for PawsOff

**Effective Date:** August 28, 2026

### 1. Data Collection
PawsOff operates entirely locally on your device. We **do not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, browsing history, or user activity data with external servers or third parties. 

### 2. Permissions Used
- **Storage:** Used solely to save your personal preferences (such as the list of sites you wish to block and time limits) locally on your device.
- **ActiveTab / Web Navigation:** Used strictly to detect when you are visiting a site on your blocklist so the extension can display the overlay. This detection happens entirely locally within your browser.

### 3. Third-Party Services
PawsOff does not use any third-party analytics, trackers, or remote code. 

### 4. Contact
If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository:
