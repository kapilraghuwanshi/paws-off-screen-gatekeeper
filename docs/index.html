How It Works

Three steps to a healthier browsing habit

1

Install & Open Settings

Add PawsOff from the Chrome Web Store in one click. Click the paw icon to open your settings popup.

2

Pick Your Sites & Limits

Add the sites you want to guard — Reddit, Twitter, YouTube — and choose how long you're allowed before the puppy shows up.

3

Get Pawed 🐾

When time's up, an adorable dog walks onto your screen and counts down your break. The page unlocks automatically when it ends.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

🎨

Gorgeous Overlay

A full-screen glassmorphism overlay with an animated puppy that walks in and sits with you during your break.

⏱

Custom Timers

Set your usage limit from 1 to 60 minutes, and choose how long your puppy break lasts. Fully configurable.

🌐

Per-Site Blocking

Only guard the sites you choose. Add Reddit, Twitter, YouTube — or any custom URL — from the settings popup.

🔒

100% Private

All settings are stored locally on your device. Zero data is ever collected, tracked, or transmitted. Period.

⚡

Lightweight

No frameworks, no trackers, no telemetry. Pure vanilla JS that's fast and doesn't slow your browser down.
